# EverCare generated health assets

Generation mode: OpenAI built-in image generation, using project-owned
EverCare artwork as visual references. Production files are optimized copies;
the original generated outputs remain in the Codex generated-images folder.

## Shared BP hero direction

Create a premium 2:1 mobile health-card watercolor/gouache illustration based
on EverCare's existing `bp_result_care_v1.png` and app icon. Keep the same warm
Asian older adult and caregiver in a calm home setting, with a realistic
upper-arm cuff and a monitor whose display contains no readable numbers. Place
the people and device in the rightmost 44% and keep the left 55% pale,
low-detail EverCare green/cream negative space for UI text. Preserve rounded
card-safe margins. No embedded words, numbers, logos, diagnosis symbols,
hospital drama, fear, neon, or stock-photo styling.

State variations:

- `bp_normal_hero.webp`: relaxed posture, gentle smiles, healthy morning light,
  reassuring sage-green accents.
- `bp_elevated_hero.webp`: calmly focused expression, mild attentiveness, soft
  golden/yellow accents without alarm.
- `bp_stage1_hero.webp`: careful measurement with supportive caregiver,
  thoughtful expression, warm amber accents.
- `bp_stage2_hero.webp`: more serious but composed expressions, clear focus on
  the device, restrained warm orange/red accents.
- `bp_severe_hero.webp`: urgent but orderly support, caregiver ready with a
  phone, minimal decoration, clear warm-red emphasis without panic.
- `bp_lower_hero.webp`: older adult resting while a caregiver offers water,
  calm cool teal/blue-gray accents, no fainting or frightening symptoms.

## EverCare AI mascot direction

Create an original, centered EverCare companion based on the app icon's heart,
leaf, caring-hands, family warmth, and watercolor language. Use a cream
heart-shaped face framed by evergreen leaf-like caring hands, a small coral
heart, a gentle calm smile, and three restrained lavender sparkles for AI
identity. Use an intentional, continuous pale cream/sage watercolor wash with
no tiles, grids, or fake transparency. Keep the silhouette simple, trustworthy,
and readable on a mobile card; no text, medical cross, sci-fi robot parts, neon,
or overly childish proportions.

Variants:

- `evercare_ai_mascot_main.png`: detailed 768 px main illustration.
- `evercare_ai_mascot_icon.png`: simplified, higher-contrast 192 px small icon.
- `evercare_ai_mascot_avatar.png`: 256 px circular cream/sage medallion on a
  continuous pale sage background for clean circular clipping.
